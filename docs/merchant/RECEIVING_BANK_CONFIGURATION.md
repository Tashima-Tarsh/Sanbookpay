# Receiving Bank Configuration

Merchants can view their approved receiving bank settings:

- Approved receiving accounts
- Approved VPAs
- Bank connection status
- Transaction feed (where supported)
- Settlement status
- Reconciliation results

Merchants can request new bank connections and VPA assignments through the Platform Owner.

## Restrictions

- Cannot access another merchant's bank account
- Cannot access another merchant's credentials
- Cannot assign unauthorized receiving VPAs
- Cannot bypass Platform Owner restrictions
- Cannot change provider eligibility requirements
