# Dynamo Pay merchant console

Mode: Operate. A restrained light workspace for payment operations, with a navy sidebar and violet primary actions. System sans typography, tabular financial figures, 12px data rows, 14px body, 27px page headings. No decorative gradients or unverified service claims.

Desktop: fixed 242px left navigation, 73px top bar, content on the right with 36px gutters. Compact metric strip followed by operational tables and forms. At 760px the sidebar becomes a menu and forms stack. Tables scroll within their own container.

Surface #ffffff, canvas #f5f6fa, text #192238, secondary #626d82, border #e3e7ef, action #4b47df. Green indicates confirmed success; amber marks pending bank action; red marks unsuccessful attempts. Use consistent authored line icons, explicit labels, visible keyboard focus, and honest empty/error states.

Checkout uses the same brand and palette: navy order summary, white payment panel, official app icons, legible QR and exact payment amounts. Mobile summary becomes compact above the payment controls.
