Request a refund

Direct UPI payments require a bank transfer back to the customer. This records the refund request and reserves the amount. Confirm it only after sending the money and receiving a bank reference.

Refund process

1. Select a paid transaction
2. Record the amount and reason
3. Transfer funds through your bank
4. Record the bank reference

Partial refunds are supported. Pending and confirmed refunds reduce the remaining refundable amount.

Refund history
